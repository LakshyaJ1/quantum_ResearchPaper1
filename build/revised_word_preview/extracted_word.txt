                                                                                      VII. CONCLUSION
                                                           For finite-reservoir trusted-relay QKD, a generation cut, a
                                                               key-store policy and a service policy are separate modeling
                                                                  decisions. Across the tested graphs, fallback can preserve
                                                              timely key service while providing  little additional floor-
                                                           compliant service. Common arrival denominators and explicit
                                                                    class  outcomes  make  that  distinction  measurable.  The
                                                      accompanying  cut-budget  fixtures,  parameter sweeps and
                                                               constrained policy study provide a reproducible basis for
                                                               evaluating the trade-off under stated assumptions.
