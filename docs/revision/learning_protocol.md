# Registered learning experiment protocol

This protocol is specified before training or final evaluation. Implementation uses the frozen corrected simulator, revision scenarios, the 18-state/five-action BudgetedTablePolicy, and the common-denominator utility already defined in revision.metrics. The work is an interpretable, bounded policy-search comparison; no learned-policy advantage or global optimality is assumed. Training and evaluation are not launched as part of implementation verification.

The implementation plan is to test the split and selection contracts first, implement immutable rollout caching and complete accounting records, implement CEM with explicit incumbent candidates, choose restarts only on validation, freeze tables plus their full deployment test plan, and permit final evaluation only after verifying that freeze. The owned files are experiments/revision_learning.py, tests/test_revision_learning.py and this protocol. Core simulator, policy, scenario and metric files are dependencies and are not modified by this task.

## Fixed policy and search space

The state is the frozen three-class by three floor-feasibility by two urgency representation. Actions are floor, affordable fallback, defer, direct G4 and direct G2. Every searched policy uses the same runtime exception projection: C2 zero exceptions, ISR and LOG at most 0.1 of their respective arrivals at every observed prefix. These are explicit contracts, not reward penalties. The table of all zeroes reproduces StrictFloorPolicy; all ones reproduces BudgetGreedyPolicy. Those equalities are verified with real small simulations.

There are three training regimes: short_nominal uses the nominal case with a 21600-second arrival horizon; full_nominal uses the nominal 86400-second case; full_robust uses three 86400-second conditions. All have warmup zero and the existing deadline drain. These are finite-event experiments; none is labelled steady state.

Each regime has two independent CEM restarts with optimizer seeds 7000 and 7001, paired across regimes. Each restart runs four generations of ten unique candidate tables. Every population includes the all-zero table, all-one table, and previous incumbent, with duplicates collapsed and the remaining slots sampled from independent per-state categorical probabilities. The first incumbent is all ones. Probabilities start uniformly at 0.2. The three highest-ranked candidates are elites. Each state/action count receives a fixed 0.5 pseudocount before row normalization for the next generation. Incumbents rank by mean training utility, then the number of action changes between adjacent table cells, then lexicographic table order. This low budget is intentionally limited: a selected table is the best observed under the registered search and validation procedure, not an optimum.

## Realization splits and objective

Each candidate has six training rollouts. Both nominal regimes use seeds 1000 through 1005 on their respective nominal horizon. Robust training assigns nominal seeds 1000/1001, a generation attack at budget two seeds 1002/1003, and severe correlated-generation outage plus half rate seeds 1004/1005. Severe weather has mean uptime 900 seconds and mean outage 600 seconds; transport assumptions otherwise retain the Case defaults. All candidates in a condition see the same exogenous realization.

The objective is the arithmetic mean of utility A minus 0.75 times the degraded-arrival fraction over exactly those six rollouts. Thus the robust objective averages the three equally represented conditions. It is not minimax optimization. Condition means and the minimum condition utility are reported as diagnostics, together with worst condition availability, qualified availability, degradation and blocking. Per-class raw outcomes remain available for interpretation.

The nominal requested search budget is 10 candidates × 4 generations × 2 restarts × 6 rollouts = 480 candidate-rollout requests per regime, or 1440 across all three regimes. Deterministic repeat/incumbent rollouts are cached, so actual unique simulations can be fewer; both requested and executed/reused counts are reported. The nominal regimes have the same number of rollouts but different simulated hours, which is the intended horizon intervention. Restart validation adds two candidates × three conditions × four seeds = 24 requested rollouts per regime, 72 total before cache reuse.

Validation always uses the full 86400-second horizon and the three nominal/attack/severe conditions, each on seeds 2000 through 2003. A restart is selected by the largest mean validation utility across all twelve paired condition/seed cells. Exact ties prefer fewer adjacent action changes, then the lexicographic table, then lower restart index. Validation data must contain every registered pair exactly once; training or final-test seeds are rejected by the selection function.

No seeds 5000 through 5011 may be used for training, tuning, or restart selection. They are reserved for the frozen final test. If a policy, state/action representation, reward, budget, condition or selection rule is changed after final-test inspection, that produces a new exploratory study and requires an explicitly separate protocol/output location rather than silently refreshing the existing freeze.

## Final freeze and test

After all three restart selections, results/revision/learning_policy_freeze.json records each selected raw table, its SHA-256, optimizer seed, training and validation summaries, validation rollout references, source/runtime fingerprint, protocol digest, and the complete final-test plan. The freeze has its own integrity hash. Existing freezes cannot be overwritten by training. Test evaluation requires an intact freeze and an unchanged scientific source/runtime fingerprint and registered test plan. A test-only run cannot produce or select a policy.

The test conditions are nominal, generation attack budget two, ISR overload with scale one, half-rate operation, severe correlated weather without the extra half-rate change, an unseen eight-node ring, and an unseen three-parallel-path topology with generation attack budget three. Each runs for 86400 seconds with warmup zero on seeds 5000 through 5011. The five compared policies are StrictFloorPolicy, BudgetGreedyPolicy and the three frozen selected tables: 7 × 12 × 5 = 420 requested test rollouts. Identical learned tables may share cached numerical evaluations but retain their regime labels in the report. No additional unrestricted-fallback comparator is enabled in this bounded protocol.

## Provenance and implementation safeguards

Each cached rollout is keyed by stage, condition, full Case configuration, policy kind/table/budgets, seed and scientific source/runtime fingerprint. The fingerprint covers all qkdsim modules, revision/scenarios.py, revision/policies.py, revision/metrics.py and the learning script, plus Python/NumPy/NetworkX versions. OS metadata uses os.name and sys.getwindowsversion, avoiding the reproducible native crash caused here by platform.platform().

Every rollout saves all-class and per-class summaries, raw numerator/denominator counters, arrival-trace hash, full accounting, conservation residual, policy budget snapshot, independently reconstructed arrival-prefix exception audit, and runtime. Missing conditional quantities are JSON null, with their raw counts retained. Nonfinite overall utility is rejected during scoring rather than silently imputed. Rollout records and the freeze carry integrity hashes; mismatched source or corrupted cache entries are rejected.

Candidate jobs are deduplicated before executor submission. Atomic writes use unique temporary filenames so repeat candidates cannot collide on a shared .tmp path. A single coordinator owns the run manifest and search histories. Histories save complete population tables, before/after categorical probabilities, every candidate's score and rollout references, incumbent, optimizer RNG state and requested search budget. Restart replay is deterministic and can reuse verified rollout caches after an interruption.

The final budget audit independently orders every arrival before any completion at the same timestamp and checks cumulative below-floor completions against floor(epsilon × arrivals) using rational arithmetic. This audit covers the full run including deadline drain; it does not infer real-world cryptographic compromise from a lower grade. The guarantee still comes from the fixed runtime projection and its assumptions, not from CEM.

## Lightweight verification boundary

Unit tests exercise disjoint seeds, matched training budgets, deterministic candidate generation, incumbent inclusion, categorical elite updates, validation-only restart selection, missing/foreign seed rejection, freeze tampering/source mismatch, strict JSON null conversion, pre-dispatch deduplication, cache reuse, budget audit, and equivalence of constant tables to named baselines. Real verification fixtures are 240-second toy cases stored only in temporary directories. They assert no empirical superiority and do not execute or save any registered training, validation or final-test rollout.

Implementation verification completed with `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_revision_learning.py -v`: thirteen tests passed. The initial tests were observed failing before the relevant implementation existed. `python -m py_compile experiments/revision_learning.py tests/test_revision_learning.py` and whitespace checks also completed successfully. `python experiments/revision_learning.py --describe` prints the protocol and source fingerprints without creating any experiment directory or freeze. At readiness handoff, neither results/revision/learning nor learning_policy_freeze.json existed; no training result is claimed by this implementation report.

Launch training when compute capacity is available using `python experiments/revision_learning.py --phase train --workers 6`. Inspect the completed training/validation histories and the frozen tables before running `python experiments/revision_learning.py --phase test --workers 6`. Inspection may identify a bug requiring a separately versioned rerun, but must not be used to tune the protocol against final-test outcomes. The all phase performs these steps sequentially and writes the freeze before it enters test evaluation.
