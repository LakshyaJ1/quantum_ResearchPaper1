# Final document pipeline audit

This audit records the archived five-page delivery baseline and the gates required for an exactly six-page IEEE manuscript with an editable Word counterpart. The first assessment did not edit manuscript or delivery files. Subsequent authorized pipeline corrections are recorded below; a completed six-page delivery is not yet claimed here.

## Archived five-page baseline

The archived `Paper167_Manuscript.pdf` and `Paper167_LaTeX.pdf` each had **five pages**, with embedded fonts. The former was the Microsoft Word export of `Paper167_Manuscript.docx` and the page-reference authority. That DOCX was a valid OOXML ZIP with 82 native Office Math elements, including six display equations, three drawings, and one research table plus six equation-layout tables. Its `docProps/app.xml` cached `Pages=1`, which was stale and cannot establish actual Word pagination. The prior Word QA recorded 3,619 prose/numeric tokens with no omissions, 13 references, three figures, editable tables, matching author block, and full visual review, but those findings apply only to the archived source and artifact hashes (`paper.tex` prefix `740a8458`, DOCX `11c40920`, Word PDF `7d4aa184`). The archived reviewer-compliance PDF had ten pages and was tied to that old Word-PDF hash. The conference email permits four to six pages, while the present task specifically requires **exactly six**.

The Word document was produced with the repository's Pandoc 3.11 converter and an isolated hidden Microsoft Word 16 export. This layout is derived from IEEEtran A4 geometry; `docs/revision/build_environment.md` explicitly says it is not a downloaded official IEEE Word template. The documented bundled dependency loader is unavailable in this session, and the managed DOCX renderer would select the user's installed LibreOffice on Windows. The prior Windows pipeline used the documented Python, Pandoc, Tectonic, and isolated Word fallback. Do not use the user's installed LibreOffice. The local PDF is a **candidate for PDF eXpress**, not a certified PDF eXpress output; neither embedded fonts nor local visual inspection confers certification.

## Six-page gates and correction status

The original `tools/verify_packaged_manuscript.py` asserted five pages and expected a five-page pixel match. The original `tools/build_delivery.py` fixed the LaTeX visual-review list and packaged-page list at five pages and asserted a five-page Word PDF. These two scripts have now been updated to require six pages, current artifact hashes, a six-page extracted-source rebuild, and an explicit hash-bound LaTeX visual-review record. `build/final_word/audit_conversion.py` now derives research-table and equation counts from the fresh converter manifest and Word XML, requiring three research tables, six equation-layout rows, and a six-page Word PDF before writing its technical report. No final build or visual-review outcome is inferred from these script edits.

The remaining reviewer and narrative files need final pagination reconciliation. `build/reviewer_compliance/verify_pdf.py` still requires the literal phrase `authoritative five-page manuscript PDF`. `docs/revision/REVIEWER_RESPONSE.md` and `docs/revision/official_review_matrix.md` contain five-page locators, and the generated reviewer-compliance material uses that basis. `MANUSCRIPT_BUILD.md`, `submission/READ_ME.md`, and root `README.md` describe the old five-page deliverable. Their revisions must follow inspection of the final Word-exported PDF.

The full-package builder first recreates `Manuscript_Source.zip` and then compares its hash with the pre-existing `results/revision/verification/packaged_manuscript.json`. After a source change, a direct `build_delivery.py` run will fail at this stale archive-hash check after already mutating the source ZIP. Rebuild the source archive, run the extracted-source verification against the new LaTeX PDF, then complete the delivery build, or refactor that ordering so the verification is generated within one controlled run. Its ZIP writer sorts members, uses a fixed member timestamp, checks CRC and every member SHA-256, and rejects source symlinks and paths outside the workspace. Final archive reproducibility must be rechecked from the new package; the old success report is version-specific.

`docs/revision/explainer_delivery_verification.json` also records protected hashes for the old paper, DOCX, and Word PDF, but `build_delivery.py` checks only the guide's own source/output hashes. Thus an unchanged 19-page guide can pass the delivery script with stale manuscript provenance. Review its claims and refresh its protected-file evidence for the final artifacts; rebuild the guide if its actual content must change.

## Recommended build and release order

Freeze the final manuscript content and numerical inputs first. Keep `paper.tex`, `paper_revised.tex`, `paper_extended.tex`, and `abstract_new.tex` synchronized according to their documented roles. Compile the final `paper.tex` with the repository's Tectonic helper into a **fresh** build directory, retain its AUX and log, and require exactly six A4 LaTeX pages if `Paper167_LaTeX.pdf` remains a delivered representation. Check unresolved references, missing glyphs, font warnings, and visible layout on all six pages.

Convert that exact source with its newly generated AUX through `tools/convert_manuscript_word.py`, then export the resulting DOCX through `tools/prepare_manuscript_tools.py --render-word`. Require both Word's repaginated count and the exported PDF count to equal six; treat the PDF exported from this exact DOCX as the authoritative local manuscript. Recompute source/input/AUX/DOCX/PDF hashes. Check author text, the full abstract and conclusions, every equation symbol and number, citations and all 13 bibliography entries, data table cells, all three figure rasters and captions, and manuscript claims against their source. The existing token-count and structural tests are helpful but cannot replace checking text order and actual rendered pages. Render and inspect **every page** of the final Word PDF at full resolution, including column flow, figure/table placement, spacing, clipping, missing glyphs, and the sixth page. Reinspect after any change; no prior five-page visual approval carries forward.

Only after the final Word PDF is frozen, remap all page references in the reviewer response and official matrix against that exact six-page PDF. Rebuild `Reviewer_Compliance.pdf`; update its verifier's reference-basis phrase and its manifest hashes, then inspect and technically verify its complete final render. Update the descriptive build/handoff documents, the six-page assertions and lists, the Word and LaTeX QA records, the guide's protected hashes, and the delivery manifest. Regenerate and verify both source archives, then validate `SHA256SUMS.txt` and the full-package link targets. Archive checks should establish that the extracted final manuscript source rebuilds the final six-page LaTeX reference; Word/PDF agreement needs its own content and rendering evidence because two different renderers will not produce pixel-identical pages.

The final conference set is the reviewer-compliance PDF, editable final Word manuscript, and **the output actually approved by IEEE PDF eXpress** from the corresponding final PDF. The currently local `Paper167_Manuscript.pdf` remains uncertified until that external step. Any later author or content change invalidates the matching export, reviewer-response locators, hashes, and certification basis; repeat those gates before CMT upload.
